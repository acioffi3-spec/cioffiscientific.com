Mass spectrometry-driven proteomics for drug discovery

I build the assays that tell a drug program whether to advance: cellular target engagement, proteome-wide selectivity, protein degradation, and pharmacodynamic biomarkers in clinical samples. I developed these platforms over 9+ years across covalent and induced-proximity programs at multiple biotech companies.

Through Cioffi Scientific, I help small biotech companies advance programs through key milestones, and I advise life-science AI teams on improving models for drug discovery.
